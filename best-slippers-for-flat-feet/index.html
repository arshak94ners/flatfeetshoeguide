Why house shoes matter more than you think

Many people with flat feet spend 10+ hours a day at home, and much of that on hard floors. Going barefoot puts the full load of standing on an arch with no support, and flimsy slippers aren't much better. For people with plantar fasciitis, those first barefoot steps out of bed are often the most painful of the day.

A supportive slipper by the bed and the door means your feet get some support across the whole day, not just while you're out. It's one of the easiest habit changes there is, and much cheaper than a new pair of shoes.

What makes a slipper supportive

  • A contoured footbed with a raised arch area, not a flat foam sheet.
  • A heel cup that cradles the heel, so it doesn't roll inward.
  • An adjustable strap so the foot stays on the footbed without your toes gripping.
  • A firm sole that doesn't fold in half when you bend it.

Frequently asked questions

Is walking barefoot bad for flat feet?

Short periods barefoot on soft surfaces can help build foot strength, but standing and walking barefoot on hard floors for hours often aggravates arch and heel pain for people with flat feet. A supportive slipper for most of your time at home is a sensible middle ground.

Do slippers with arch support help plantar fasciitis?

They can help, especially for the painful first steps in the morning. Keeping a supportive slipper beside the bed means you never take those steps barefoot. For persistent heel pain, see a podiatrist.

Can I wear Vionic slippers outside?

The Gemma has a durable outsole designed for indoor and outdoor use, and Orthofeet's slippers are also built for quick trips outside. Lighter terrycloth slides like the Vionic Relax are best kept indoors.
