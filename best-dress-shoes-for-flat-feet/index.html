Best Dress Shoes for Flat Feet (Men's and Women's)

Traditional dress shoes are about the worst thing you can put on a flat foot: thin, flat, unstructured soles and no room for an insert. The good news is that a few brands now hide genuine arch support inside shoes you can wear to an office or a wedding.

How to find a dress shoe that won't wreck flat feet

  • A removable insole, or a real orthotic footbed. Most dress shoes have a thin, glued-in liner. Either buy a shoe with support built in (Vionic, Orthofeet) or one whose insole comes out, so an orthotic fits (Rockport).
  • A firm heel counter. Even a dress shoe should hold your heel upright. Soft, collapsible backs let a flat foot roll in.
  • Laces or a strap over a slip-on. Adjustable closures hold the foot against the footbed. Loose slip-ons make your toes grip to keep the shoe on.
  • A low, wide heel. For women's shoes, under 2 inches with a broad base is the comfortable zone. Platforms help by reducing the effective angle.
  • Enough depth. If you'll add an orthotic, try the shoe with it in. Many slim dress shoes simply don't have the room.

Already own dress shoes you love?

A slim, three-quarter-length orthotic can turn a comfortable-but-flat dress shoe into a supportive one, without needing as much depth as a full-length insert. Frequently asked questions

Can I wear high heels with flat feet?

Occasionally, yes, but choose a lower, wider heel (or a platform that reduces the effective height) and save them for shorter events. High heels push weight onto the forefoot and don't support a collapsing arch, so they tend to be the least comfortable choice for flat feet.

Do orthotics fit in dress shoes?

Slim or three-quarter-length orthotics fit in many dress shoes, especially ones with a removable insole. Full-length sports orthotics usually need more depth than a dress shoe has. Always try the shoe on with the orthotic in place.

Are loafers bad for flat feet?

Plain loafers with a flat, thin sole offer very little support. Loafers with a built-in orthotic footbed and a firm heel cup, like the Vionic Uptown, are a much better option for flat feet.
