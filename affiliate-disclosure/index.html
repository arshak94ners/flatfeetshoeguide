Last updated: July 17, 2026

What this means for you

Some links on this site — including "Check Price on Amazon" buttons — are affiliate links. If you click one of these links and make a qualifying purchase, we may earn a small commission. This does not change the price you pay. Amazon's pricing is the same whether you use our link or navigate to the product directly.

How this affects our recommendations

It doesn't. Our review process and ratings are based on first-hand testing and research, not on commission rates. We do not accept payment from brands in exchange for positive reviews, and we do not manipulate ratings to favor higher-commission products. When a shoe has a real drawback, we say so, regardless of how it affects a potential sale.

Why we do this

Running an independent site takes time — research, testing, writing, and maintaining accurate content as products change. Affiliate commissions help cover that time and keep the site free and ad-light for readers. We believe this is a fair, transparent way to fund independent reviews.

As required by the FTC's guidance on endorsements and testimonials, we disclose our affiliate relationship on any page containing affiliate links, including this notice and short reminders near relevant buttons and product sections.
